PhoneRelay

A desktop app for Linux that connects to your Android phone over KDE Connect: read and send texts, see notifications, browse your phone's photos and files, and share the clipboard.

Google Contacts

If you connect a Google account in Settings, PhoneRelay reads your Google contacts to show names and photos in your chats, and when you rename a contact in PhoneRelay it updates that contact in Google Contacts so your phone shows the new name too. Everything runs on your own computer.
