Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

PhoneRelay is a desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no servers, collects no analytics, and does not send your data to its developer or to anyone else.

Google user data

When you choose to connect a Google account, PhoneRelay asks for access to your Google Contacts (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts scope) and uses it only to:

  • read your contacts' names, phone numbers and photos, to show names and pictures next to your texts;
  • update a contact's name, or create a contact, when you rename someone in PhoneRelay, so the change reaches your phone.

This data is fetched directly from Google to your computer and stored only there (in your user folder, under ~/.local/share/phonelink), together with the sign-in token Google issues. It is never sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the features above.

PhoneRelay's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your phone's data

Texts, notifications, photos and contacts from your phone travel over your local network through KDE Connect, between your phone and your computer only, and are stored on your computer.

Removing access and data

Disconnect Google in PhoneRelay's Settings, or remove "PhoneRelay" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the folder ~/.local/share/phonelink removes everything PhoneRelay stored.
